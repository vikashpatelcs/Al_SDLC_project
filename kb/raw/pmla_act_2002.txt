THE PREVENTION OF MONEY-LAUNDERING ACT, 2002
ACT NO. 15 OF 2003
MINISTRY OF FINANCE, GOVERNMENT OF INDIA

CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY
Section 1: Short Title, Extent and Commencement
(1) This Act may be called the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002.
(2) It extends to the whole of India.

CHAPTER IV: OBLIGATIONS OF BANKING COMPANIES, FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS AND INTERMEDIARIES
Section 12: Reporting Entity to Maintain Records
(1) Every reporting entity shall:
    (a) maintain a record of all transactions, including information relating to transactions referred to in clause (b) in such manner as to enable it to reconstruct individual transactions;
    (b) furnish to the Director Financial Intelligence Unit - India (FIU-IND) information relating to suspicious transactions (STR) and cash transactions (CTR) within the prescribed time limit;
    (c) verify the identity of its clients and the beneficial owner in such manner and subject to such conditions as may be prescribed;
    (d) maintain record of documents evidencing identity of its clients and beneficial owners as well as account files and business correspondence relating to its clients.

(2) Every information maintained, furnished or verified under sub-section (1) shall be kept confidential.

Section 12(3) & 12(4): Statutory Retention Period
(3) The records referred to in clause (a) of sub-section (1) relating to transactions shall be maintained for a period of five years from the date of transaction between a client and the reporting entity.
(4) The records referred to in clause (e) of sub-section (1) relating to identity of clients and beneficial owners shall be maintained for a period of five years after the business relationship between a client and the reporting entity has ended or the account has been closed, whichever is later.

Section 12AA: Enhanced Due Diligence
(1) Every reporting entity shall, prior to the commencement of each specified transaction:
    (a) verify the identity of the client undertaking such specified transaction by authentication under the Aadhaar Act or other officially valid documents;
    (b) take additional steps to examine the background and purpose of the transaction where the transaction value is high or inconsistent with the client profile.
(2) Where client identity does not match or transaction is suspected to involve proceeds of crime, the reporting entity shall not allow the transaction and immediately file a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) with FIU-IND.
