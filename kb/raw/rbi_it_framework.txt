RESERVE BANK OF INDIA
DEPARTMENT OF NON-BANKING SUPERVISION & BANKING SUPERVISION
MUMBAI

Master Direction - Information Technology Framework for the Financial Sector
Circular Ref: RBI/DNBS/2016-17/45 Master Direction DNBS.PPD.01/66.15.001/2016-17
Updated November 2023

SECTION I: IT GOVERNANCE AND STRATEGY
1.1 Board Oversight and IT Strategy Committee
Regulated Entities shall institute an IT Strategy Committee headed by an independent director. The committee shall ensure alignment of IT investments with enterprise risk management, statutory auditability, and regulatory mandates.

1.2 System Architecture and Change Management
All financial applications shall have documented architecture diagrams, interface definitions, and strict change management policies. No production modification shall be deployed without independent testing and rollback verification.

SECTION II: BUSINESS CONTINUITY AND DISASTER RECOVERY
2.1 Business Continuity Plan (BCP)
(a) Regulated Entities shall formulate a comprehensive Business Continuity Plan (BCP) covering critical operational services and payment transaction clearing.
(b) BCP drills shall be conducted at least once every six months to validate failover readiness.

2.2 Recovery Point Objective (RPO) and Recovery Time Objective (RTO)
(a) For core financial and transaction processing systems, the Recovery Point Objective (RPO) shall be near-zero, ensuring zero committed transaction loss.
(b) The Recovery Time Objective (RTO) shall not exceed two hours for mission-critical core banking and payment processing services.
(c) The Primary Data Centre (PDC) and Disaster Recovery Site (DRS) must be geographically separated in different seismic zones.

SECTION III: AUDIT TRAILS AND LOG RETENTION
3.1 Audit Trail Immutability
All core banking and payment applications must maintain an automated, tamper-evident audit trail for every user action, transaction record modification, and system privilege escalation.

3.2 Log Fields and Storage
(a) Audit logs must capture timestamp with millisecond precision synchronized via Network Time Protocol (NTP), unique user ID, source IP address, terminal identifier, and before/after values of modified state.
(b) System audit logs must be written to write-once-read-many (WORM) storage or append-only secure repositories and preserved for a minimum period of 3 years.
