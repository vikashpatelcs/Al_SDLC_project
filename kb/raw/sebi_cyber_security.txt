SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA
MARKET INTERMEDIARIES REGULATION AND SUPERVISION DEPARTMENT
MUMBAI

Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework for Market Intermediaries
Circular Ref: SEBI/HO/MIRSD/CIR/P/2022/65
May 20, 2022

SECTION I: ACCESS CONTROL AND CLIENT AUTHENTICATION
1.1 Mandatory Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for Trading
(a) All stock brokers, depository participants, and algorithmic trading systems shall mandate Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for login to internet-based trading applications.
(b) The 2FA mechanism must combine knowledge factor (password/PIN) and possession factor (OTP, authenticator token) or inherence factor (biometric fingerprint/face).
(c) Session timeouts shall automatically terminate idle trading sessions after 15 minutes of inactivity.

SECTION II: NETWORK ISOLATION AND CRYPTOGRAPHIC DATA INTEGRITY
2.1 Isolation of Trading Rails and Demat Databases
(a) Core order matching engines, depository ledger databases, and client financial ledgers must reside within isolated demilitarized zones (DMZ) with no direct internet ingress.
(b) Database connections must require encrypted transport and mutual TLS (mTLS) with client certificates.
(c) Data at rest containing investor portfolios, PANs, demat account numbers, and bank mandates must be encrypted using AES-256 standards.

SECTION III: VULNERABILITY ASSESSMENT AND CYBER AUDIT
3.1 Periodic VAPT and Third-Party Audits
(a) Regulated market intermediaries shall conduct Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) at least once every six months through CERT-In empanelled auditing agencies.
(b) Annual comprehensive cyber security audits must be submitted to SEBI along with board approval and remedial action plans within 60 days of fiscal year end.

SECTION IV: INCIDENT REPORTING TO SEBI
4.1 Cyber Incident Escalation Timeline
Any cyber attack, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) incident, unauthorized access, or loss of investor data must be reported to SEBI and CERT-In within 4 hours of detection via the prescribed portal.
