Master Direction - Credit Card and Debit Card – Issuance and Conduct Directions, 2022
Circular Ref: RBI/2022-23/92 Master Direction DOR.AUT.REC.No.27/24.01.041/2022-23
April 21, 2022

CHAPTER II: CONDUCT OF CARD OPERATIONS
Section 7: Customer Consent and Card Activation
(a) Explicit customer consent shall be obtained prior to activating a credit card. If a card is not activated by the customer within 30 days of issuance, the issuer shall seek an OTP-based confirmation, failing which the account shall be closed within seven working days at zero cost.
(b) Modification of credit limits shall require explicit affirmative customer consent; automated unilateral credit limit enhancement is strictly prohibited.

Section 10: Card-on-File Tokenization (CoFT) and Credential Storage
(a) No entity in the card transaction or payment chain, other than the card issuer and the card payment network, shall store actual card details (Card Number, CVV, Expiry Date).
(b) Merchants and payment aggregators shall exclusively store and transact using encrypted Card-on-File Tokens (CoFT) provisioned through authorized token service providers.
(c) The CVV / CVC code must never be stored in persistent databases under any circumstances, even in encrypted form, after transaction authorization is complete.

Section 14: User Control and Limits
(a) Cardholders shall be provided 24x7 self-service facility through mobile banking and internet banking to turn on/off and set transaction velocity limits for:
    1. Domestic transactions;
    2. International transactions;
    3. Contactless (NFC) tap-and-pay transactions;
    4. Point-of-Sale (POS) and ATM withdrawals;
    5. E-commerce online transactions.

Section 22: Grievance Redressal and Disputed Transactions
(a) Issuers shall ensure resolution of disputed transactions and customer chargebacks within a maximum period of 30 days from date of lodging the complaint.
(b) If an issuer fails to resolve a customer complaint within 30 days, the customer may escalate the grievance to the RBI Ombudsman Scheme.
