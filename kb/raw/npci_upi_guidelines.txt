NATIONAL PAYMENTS CORPORATION OF INDIA
MUMBAI

Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Procedural Guidelines & Operating Circulars
Operating Circular Ref: NPCI/UPI/OC-87/2020-21
Updated Guidelines on UPI Security, Architecture and Limits

CHAPTER 2: PARTICIPANT ARCHITECTURE AND AUTHENTICATION RAILS
2.1 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and UPI PIN
(a) Every financial transaction initiated over the UPI rail must be authenticated by the customer using a 4-digit or 6-digit MPIN entered through the Common Library (CL) provided by NPCI.
(b) The MPIN must never be intercepted, cached, or decrypted by Payment Service Provider (PSP) apps or merchant SDKs.
(c) The CL securely encrypts the MPIN with the issuer bank's public key inside the device memory before network transmission.

CHAPTER 4: TRANSACTION CEILINGS AND VELOCITY LIMITS
4.1 Per-Transaction and Daily Limits
(a) The standard per-transaction limit for peer-to-peer (P2P) and peer-to-merchant (P2M) UPI transactions is capped at 1,00,000 INR per transaction.
(b) For verified merchants in capital markets, collections, insurance premiums, and educational institutions, the ceiling is extended up to 2,00,000 INR and 5,00,000 INR per transaction subject to risk evaluation.
(c) Participant banks must enforce daily cumulative velocity controls and reject transactions exceeding 20 transfers per 24-hour rolling window.

CHAPTER 5: OPERATIONAL RESILIENCE AND SETTLEMENT SLA
5.1 API Response Timeout
(a) The remitter and beneficiary banks must respond to authorization API requests from the NPCI UPI switch within a timeout ceiling of 3000 milliseconds (3 seconds).
(b) In the event of network timeout or pending transaction status, automated query APIs (UPI Status Check) must be executed before generating customer charge slips.

5.2 Real-Time Settlement and Auto-Reversal
(a) UPI transactions operate on 24x7 immediate gross settlement across participating member banks.
(b) In case of technical failure where customer account is debited but beneficiary is not credited, auto-reversal (T+1 turnaround) must be completed within statutory time limits, failing which participant banks shall pay customer compensation of 100 INR per day of delay under RBI circular DPSS.CO.PD.No.629/02.01.014/2019-20.
